## Verified from the real photos

| Part | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Cup | Straight cylinder, smoked translucent black, glossy; 500 ml |
| Lid | Solid glossy black, screws on; wide ring slightly wider than the cup + low dome + hinged **flip-top cap with a round finger-loop**, closing over a raised round spout |
| Front print | **ATHLETIX** — white, bold wide geometric sans-serif caps, printed **vertically, reading bottom → top**, centred, ≈ ¾ of the cup height |
| Back print | White measuring scale: ml **100 / 200 / 300 / 400 / 500** (left), oz **4 / 8 / 12 / 16** (right), «ml» and «oz» labels at the bottom |
| Side | Matte graphite stepped panel inside the wall, narrowing downward (6 steps) |
| Assembled | Cup + **2** screw-on black storage tiers below — a short one, then a taller bottom one; same diameter, flush, thin seams |
| Compact | Cup + lid only |
| Whisk | Black plastic ring with 3 spokes holding a stainless-steel conical spiral spring |
| Storage | Pill organizer with **3** sections + 2 round black storage containers |
| Parts total | 6: lid, whisk, cup, pill organizer, container, container |

Approximate proportions: compact ≈ 2 : 1 (height : diameter incl. cap), assembled ≈ 2.8 : 1.

Not verified (do not claim in prompts or slides): material (PP/Tritan), BPA-free, dishwasher-safe,
hot drinks, leak-proof, exact volumes of the storage parts, which storage part nests where.

A brand-new black 500 ml protein shaker by ATHLETIX.

  bottom one), same diameter, flush, thin seams. Compact version: cup + lid only.
- Whisk: black ring with three spokes holding a stainless-steel conical spiral spring.
- Everything is black except the white print and the steel spring. Clean gloss, crisp print,
  no dust, scratches or fingerprints.
Never: change shape or proportions, add or remove parts, add any other text, logo, sticker or pattern
(never "SPIDER BOTTLE", never "GYMPIRE"), recolour, make it clear/transparent, add handles, straps,
